Considering a Token Ring network at a bit rate of 4Mbps, and a signal propagation speed of 200m/µsec, and each interfaces introducing a delay of one bit period, find the cable length equivalent with a total of 20 interfaces on that ring. One’s interface delay = One bit period: 1/(4x106) = 0.25x10-6 sec = 250nsec.
20 interfaces delay: 20x250nsec = 5µsec.
